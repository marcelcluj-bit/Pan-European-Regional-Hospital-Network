Pan-European Regional Hospital Network (81 Nodes) & AI Bio-Defense InfrastructureExecutive SummaryThis project outlines the strategic architecture, budgeting, project management frameworks, and technical deployment models for establishing a network of 81 Regional Emergency Hospitals across all 27 European Union Member States (3 nodes per country).Each facility is designed as a state-of-the-art, Near Zero Energy Building (nZEB) regional hospital equipped with advanced AI-powered Electron Microscopy Labs (SEM) for sub-second detection of Nosocomial Infections (Healthcare-Associated Infections - HAIs) and Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR) genes. The entire infrastructure is backed by a Real-World Asset (RWA) Web3 dual-token economy ($MEDGOV and $MEDINFRA).1.
